[Visit Nutrition Calculatior](https://nutritioncalculatior.com/)

Nutrition Calculatior makes it easy to explore the nutritional content of foods. Search for food items, compare their nutrients, and see how protein, carbohydrates, and fat contribute to their calorie content.

The app also supports creating recipes and calculating their nutritional content per portion, including vitamins and minerals.
